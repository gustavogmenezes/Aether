## Decisions and contracts

- Information does not teleport between characters.
- Transmission requires a source, content, sender, recipient/audience, medium, reach, and time.
- Media include in-person, physical, social, institutional, technological, and supernatural means, as the world permits.
- Information may be delayed, distorted, incomplete, omitted, or fabricated.
- Distinguish honest error, misinterpretation, unverified rumor, deliberate lie, propaganda, and fabrication.
- Knowledge changes only when information arrives and is interpreted.
- Public channels are recorded as publication; reception is aggregated by population/area when appropriate.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
