## Decisions and contracts

- All NPCs follow universal mechanical creation rules configured by the world; narrative relevance changes simulation depth, not the legitimacy of the character sheet.
- Storytelling reference: attributes 5/4/3 with one starting point in each; skills 11/7/4; three initial specialization levels; 7 Merit points.
- Narrative qualities and flaws are distinct from mechanical Merits.
- Skill 0 applies −3 to mental tests and −1 to physical/social tests, according to the approved rule.
- Character Genesis adapts biographical stages to the nature of the entity without assuming humanity.
- Mechanical, conceptual, and biographical templates guide but do not determine.
