# Validation 14 — Culture, society, and collective identity

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Culture includes values, customs, traditions, norms, beliefs, language, art, symbols, and intercultural relationships.
- Society includes classes, groups, communities, castes, professions, religions, and minorities.
- Organizations remain separate entities.
- Individuals are not deterministic copies of culture: they may adhere, question, reject, assimilate, hybridize, or transform.
- Culture is historical, heterogeneous, and dynamic, affected by migration, trade, war, colonization, conversion, revolution, and technology.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
