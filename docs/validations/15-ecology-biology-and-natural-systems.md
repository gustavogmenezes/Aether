# Validation 15 — Ecology, biology, and natural systems

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Ecological systems have their own dynamics even without civilizations.
- Represent climate, flora/fauna, biodiversity, water/nutrients, food chains, resources, and habitability.
- Species have anatomy, physiology, life cycle, reproduction, nutrition, habitat, distribution, senses, and adaptations.
- Processes include migration, extinction, disease, predation, competition, disasters, degradation, and recovery.
- Support biological, artificial, extraterrestrial, and supernatural organisms according to world rules.
- Populations are aggregated by default; individuals receive detail when relevant.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
