# Validation 30 — Sounds, music, and sonic identity

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Sound Identity System as a complementary, optional dimension.
- Environments have soundscapes, ambient sounds, weather, activities, reverberation, and spatial characteristics.
- Characters may have vocal identities: timbre, pitch, intensity, rhythm, accent, pronunciation, and species-specific traits.
- Distinguish diegetic music (exists in the world) and extradiegetic music (accompanies the experience).
- Sound events may convey information perceptible to characters according to their senses.
- Future architecture may support soundtracks, effects, voice synthesis, and adaptive audio; audio is not required for the core to function.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
