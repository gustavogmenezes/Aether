# Validation 20 — Health, medicine, and conditions of existence

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Health includes physical/biological integrity, psychology, disease, infection, injury, disability, aging, recovery, and rehabilitation.
- Physiology is specific to species/nature.
- Medicine depends on knowledge, techniques, drugs, professionals, institutions, equipment, infrastructure, and resources.
- Collective health includes epidemics, mortality, life expectancy, sanitation, nutrition, and access.
- Physical condition, consciousness, and ability to fight are distinct dimensions.
- A combatant may be injured and conscious, unconscious without severe injury, or conscious but incapacitated.
- Combat outcome does not automatically determine final medical condition; the Combat Resolution Engine produces states such as pressured, incapacitated, unconscious, surrendered, fled, or dead and feeds the Health System.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
