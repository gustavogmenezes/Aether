# Validation 26 — Transportation and mobility

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Terrestrial, aquatic, aerial, rail, space, magical/supernatural, and biological transportation.
- Infrastructure networks, capacity, speed, range, consumption, cost, maintenance, safety, and availability.
- Travel may involve an individual, group, caravan, fleet, army, migration, or evacuation.
- Integrate with the World Clock: travel has a start, duration, and end; events may occur during transit.
- Mobility depends on geography, infrastructure, technology, resources, environment, and politics.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
