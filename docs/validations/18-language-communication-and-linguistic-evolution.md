# Validation 18 — Language, communication, and linguistic evolution

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Languages have origins, families, dialects, evolution, writing systems, and regional/social variation.
- Consider language barriers, imperfect translation, dead/ancestral languages, jargon, slang, accents, and semantic changes.
- Support artificial, alien, and supernatural languages.
- Distinguish the diegetic language spoken from the language used to present dialogue to the player.
- Presentation in English does not mean that characters speak English inside the world.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
