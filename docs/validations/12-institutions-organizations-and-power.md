# Validation 12 — Institutions, organizations, and power

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Organizations are first-class entities, not merely groups of NPCs.
- They have identity, purpose, history, hierarchy, authority, rules, governance, resources, territory, goals, interests, and relationships.
- Distinguish formal authority from effective influence.
- Power dimensions may be political/legal, economic, military, social/cultural, territorial, religious/ideological, and administrative.
- Organizations may outlive founders, split, merge, change purpose, or disappear.
- Internal conflicts and personal interests prevent treating them as a homogeneous collective mind.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
