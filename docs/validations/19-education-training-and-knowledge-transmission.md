# Validation 19 — Education, training, and knowledge transmission

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Formal and informal learning, mentoring, practice, self-study, cultural transmission, and exceptional means.
- Educational institutions have curricula, methods, teachers, students, resources, infrastructure, admission criteria, and interests.
- Learning depends on aptitude, prior knowledge, cognition, motivation, time, teaching quality, practice, and resources.
- Access is unequal by class, money, location, language, species, gender where applicable, politics, and discrimination.
- Education provides opportunity, not automatic mastery.
- Distinguish education, knowledge, competence, and progression.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
