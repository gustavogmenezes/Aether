# Validation 11 — Resources, economy, and needs

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Resources are material, social/human, structural, or personal.
- Resources concretely constrain the plans and actions of NPCs and organizations.
- Needs vary by species/nature: food, rest, safety, belonging, recognition, power, etc.
- NPCs and organizations produce, consume, trade, accumulate, and lose resources.
- The economy should be abstracted by relevance rather than simulate every coin of every inhabitant.
- The economy is directly tied to region and political context: institutions, geography, resources, trade, sanctions, war, inequality, and policies change prices and availability.
- Economic credibility supports narrative; the goal is not to create a specialized economic simulator.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
