# Validation 02 — Separation of state, knowledge, and disclosure

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Separate World State, Character Knowledge, Player Knowledge, and Narrative State.
- The world may contain hidden truths that characters do not know.
- Individual knowledge derives from perception, communication, investigation, deduction, or world-defined powers.
- Narrative State preserves scene and continuity; it is not a barrier to player actions.
- The player may act using metaknowledge; resolution respects the protagonist’s actual knowledge.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
