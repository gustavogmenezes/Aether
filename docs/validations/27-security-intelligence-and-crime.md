# Validation 27 — Security, intelligence, and crime

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Individual, property, community, institutional, territorial, national, and informational security.
- Crime results from motives, goals, resources, opportunity, perceived risks, organization, relationships, and context; it is not automatically reduced to poverty.
- Intelligence/investigation gathers information, monitors threats, protects secrets, detects infiltration, and conducts clandestine operations while respecting epistemology.
- Security institutions depend on resources, training, technology, authority, and legitimacy; they may also be threats.
- Threats may be resolved through investigation, negotiation, escape, arrest, exposure, or dismantling, not only combat.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
