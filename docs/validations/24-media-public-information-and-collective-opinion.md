# Validation 24 — Media, public information, and collective opinion

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Public channels include oral/messengers, publications, radio/TV, digital networks, institutional channels, and alien, magical, or supernatural means.
- Each channel has reach, speed, reliability, cost, control, and interception potential.
- Public information may differ from objective truth.
- Collective opinion is not identical to published information; groups react according to culture, education, class, experience, trust, identity, and interests.
- Propaganda, disinformation, censorship, and manipulation may produce real effects even after correction.
- Publication is recorded; population reception may be aggregated.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
