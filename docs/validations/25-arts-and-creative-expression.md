# Validation 25 — Arts and creative expression

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Art and entertainment include music, dance, literature, poetry, visual arts, theater, cinema, architecture, design, crafts, games, and species-specific forms.
- Works have a creator, context, style, tradition, materials, technique, intent, audience, meaning, and influence.
- Art preserves memory, questions institutions, reinforces identities, disseminates ideologies, and expresses conflict.
- Censorship and repression are possible.
- The creative economy includes patronage, trade, institutions, contracts, and audiences.
- Not every work needs historical relevance; people also create for pleasure and expression.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
