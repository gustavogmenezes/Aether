# Validation 17 — Religion, spirituality, and belief systems

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Separate individual belief, religious institution, and objective metaphysical reality.
- Traditions have cosmology, doctrines, entities/principles, rituals, texts, values, and internal interpretations.
- Religious institutions are organizations with leadership, hierarchy, resources, influence, and politics.
- Objective metaphysics is defined by the world; characters do not know it automatically.
- Institutional membership and individual faith are not equivalent.
- Religions change, expand, split, reform, or disappear.
- Metaphysical truth belongs to the world; interpretation belongs to characters and societies.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
