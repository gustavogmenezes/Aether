# Validation 28 — Entertainment, leisure, and everyday life

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Everyday life includes meals, rest, recreation, sports, games/gambling, festivals, gatherings, leisure travel, domestic activities, and hobbies.
- Social spaces include taverns, cafés, restaurants, squares, parks, clubs, theaters, sports facilities, and community venues.
- NPCs have routines, commitments, preferences, work, family, leisure, and rest.
- Leisure creates friendship, rivalry, romance, competition, recognition, skills, and community; not everything needs to generate a mechanical reward.
- Tier abstraction: detailed primary NPCs, secondary NPCs with abstract routines, populations with statistical patterns.
- The world should remain interesting when nothing extraordinary is happening.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
