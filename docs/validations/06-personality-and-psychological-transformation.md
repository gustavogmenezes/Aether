## Decisions and contracts

- Distinguish identity, personality, beliefs/values, behavior, and emotional state.
- Personality is not immutable; profound and even contradictory changes are possible.
- Transformations must be supported by experiences, events, and a causal trajectory.
- Do not permit arbitrary personality rewrites without evidence.
