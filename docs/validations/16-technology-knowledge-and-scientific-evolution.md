# Validation 16 — Technology, knowledge, and scientific evolution

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Knowledge may be scientific, technical, practical, philosophical, cultural, or supernatural/magical.
- Distinguish knowledge that exists, has been discovered, is documented, is transmitted, is mastered, and is applied.
- Technologies require theory, materials, manufacture, infrastructure, labor, funding, and political conditions.
- Distribution depends on language, secrets, infrastructure, censorship, cost, and power.
- Evolution is non-linear; knowledge may diverge, be lost, or be rediscovered.
- Advanced technology does not imply automatic secularization. Science and religion interact in both directions according to world history and evidence.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
