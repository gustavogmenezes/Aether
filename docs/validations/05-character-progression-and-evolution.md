# Validation 05 — Character progression and evolution

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- There are no mandatory universal levels or XP.
- Evolution results from meaningful experience, difficulty, participation, learning, practice, reflection, adaptation, aptitude, and limitations.
- Progression may be positive or negative: injuries, trauma, aging, decline, and loss of capability.
- Evidence history remains associated with events, separate from the current character sheet.
- The LLM proposes; the Character Progression Engine validates and applies according to world rules.
- NPCs continue evolving while the protagonist is occupied elsewhere.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
