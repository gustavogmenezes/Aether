## Decisions and contracts

- Pipeline: perception → interpretation → reaction.
- Perception depends on senses, attributes, skills, environment, attention, condition, and nature/species.
- Interpretation depends on intelligence/knowledge, personality, experiences, beliefs, memory, and emotions.
- Reaction depends on personality, goals/plans, relationships, capabilities, and state.
- NPCs do not receive automatic access to the world’s objective state.
- Agents must produce individually coherent behavior, not arbitrary scripts.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
