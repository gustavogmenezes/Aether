# Validation 21 — Reproduction, demography, and life cycles

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Population dynamics include births, mortality, life expectancy, migration, distribution, reproduction, cycles, and age structure.
- Reproduction may be biological, artificial, or supernatural according to species.
- Population changes affect labor, succession, inheritance, resources, culture, and generations.
- War and crisis produce long-term demographic consequences.
- Relevant characters may be simulated individually; background population statistically.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
