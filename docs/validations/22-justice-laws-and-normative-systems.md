# Validation 22 — Justice, laws, and normative systems

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Support formal laws, customs, religious codes, community rules, institutional regulations, treaties, and private/supernatural systems.
- Define jurisdiction, authority, rights/duties, offenses, sanctions, investigation, trial, appeal, and enforcement.
- Distinguish legal validity, enforceability, territorial scope, oversight, corruption, social acceptance, and legitimacy.
- Systems may be unjust, discriminatory, unequal, corrupt, or politically instrumentalized.
- Characters know laws according to education, experience, profession, location, and available information.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
