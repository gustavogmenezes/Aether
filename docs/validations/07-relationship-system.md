## Decisions and contracts

- Relationships are directional: A’s relationship to B may differ from B’s relationship to A.
- Independent dimensions include trust, respect, affection, fear, admiration, resentment, loyalty, attraction, etc.
- Not every relationship needs to use every dimension.
- Relationships may be hidden, unilateral, contradictory, or conditional.
- Changes result from experiences/events and are preserved historically in the Event Ledger.
- The Engine records state/evidence; interpretation and decisions belong to the character.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
