# Validation 23 — Conflicts, diplomacy, and international relations

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Relations among states/organizations have independent dimensions and may be asymmetric.
- Diplomacy includes treaties, alliances, trade, information sharing, concessions, reparations, sanctions, political marriages, and ceasefires.
- Escalation may progress through diplomatic, economic, political, and intelligence tension, proxies, limited conflict, war, occupation, and reorganization, without a mandatory linear path.
- Strategic war considers objectives, military capability, logistics, finance, morale, command, terrain, infrastructure, public support, allies, and economic/demographic effects.
- Combat Resolution resolves tactical engagements; military strategy remains separate.
- Political entities act according to interests, resources, and context.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
