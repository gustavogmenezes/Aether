# Validation 13 — Geography, territory, and infrastructure

**Status:** APPROVED



## Decisions and contracts

- Configurable hierarchical geography, from world/planet to points of interest.
- Represent terrain, climate, hydrology, flora/fauna, resources, density, routes, barriers, strategic points, and risks.
- Distinguish location, ownership, and effective control; control may be contested, occupied, or shared.
- Infrastructure includes roads, bridges, ports, networks, hospitals, schools, energy, communications, and strategic facilities.
- Distinguish physical distance from travel time/cost.
- Geography influences economy, politics, conflict, mobility, and society.


## Implementation rule
Implementations must preserve these decisions and their cross-system contracts. Technical difficulties must be recorded as conflicts/proposals, never resolved through silent relaxation.
